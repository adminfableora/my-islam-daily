Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what My Islam Daily (“the app”), made by Fableora (“we”), collects and why.

Without an account

The app's core features work offline. Your prayer records, qaza list and settings are stored only on your phone. Your location is used on your phone to calculate prayer times and is not sent to us. The only exception is the travel-mode home location, which is included in your Pro cloud backup if you use both (see below). To show a city name or search for a city, the location may be sent to your phone's map service (Google) or to OpenStreetMap.

With an account (optional)

  • Account details: your name and email address (from Google sign-in or the email you enter).
  • Cloud backup (Pro): a copy of your prayer records, your qaza list and the data of your Pro tools — family members and their prayer marks, your masjid's jama'at times, your tasbeeh, azkar and Quran counts, and your travel-mode home location (if you set one) — so you can restore them on a new phone.
  • Plan status: whether you have Pro and until when.

This data is stored with Supabase (our database provider, servers in Japan), encrypted in transit, and protected so that only you can read your own data.

Accountability partner (optional)

If you link with a partner using an invite code, that partner can see only your display name, your current streak and how many prayers you prayed today — never which prayers you missed or any other record. You can remove a partner at any time, which stops this sharing immediately.

Family mode and auto-silent

Family members you add (and their prayer marks) are stored on your phone and, if you use Pro cloud backup, in your private backup. Auto-silent changes your phone's sound mode on your phone only; nothing is sent to us.

File backups

If you use “Back up history”, the app creates a file on your phone that you choose where to save or share (for example Google Drive or WhatsApp). We never receive this file.

Payments

Payments are processed by Cashfree Payments. We receive your mobile number, the amount and whether the payment succeeded. We never see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank details.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share your prayer records with anyone. The only thing ever shown to someone else is the short summary an accountability partner sees, and only if you link with one.

Deleting your data

You can delete the data on your phone in Settings → Erase all history. To delete your account and cloud backup, email us at help@fableora.com from your account email and we will delete it within 7 days.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Accounts and payments are meant for users aged 18 or over, or for younger users with a parent's permission.
